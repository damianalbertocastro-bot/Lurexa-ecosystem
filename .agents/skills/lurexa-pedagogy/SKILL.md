# Lurexa Pedagogical & Curriculum Skill

Use this skill when designing, implementing, or updating lessons, exercises, AI conversation flows, phonetics activities, or adaptive recommendations.

## Core Rules

1. **Seven English Skills**:
   - Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Vocabulary, Grammar, Phonetics.
   - Conversation and Create & Apply are integrated modes.
2. **Dominican Spanish to English Linguistic Transfer**:
   - Prioritize intelligibility, naturalness, fluency, pronunciation refinement, and spoken confidence.
   - Support Dominican-Spanish-to-English linguistic transfer pedagogically (e.g. vowel shifts, syllable-final consonants, rhythm/stress).
   - Phonetics optimizes for intelligibility and communicative control, NOT accent erasure.
3. **CEFR Alignment & Placement**:
   - Respect prior learner knowledge (do not force knowledgeable learners through A1).
   - Preserve first-attempt evidence separately from eventual success after hints/retries.
4. **Structured Learning Objects**:
   - Model lessons and activities using structured schemas (`Docs/Curriculum/03-LESSON-AND-ACTIVITY-SCHEMA.md`).
   - Do not hard-code curriculum copy when structured objects are applicable.

- Design pedagogical frameworks, adaptive learning paths, and scaffolded feedback mechanisms for English language learning based on `Docs/Curriculum/00-LUREXA-LEARNING-METHODOLOGY.md`.
- Support Dominican-Spanish-to-English linguistic transfer pedagogically (`Docs/Curriculum/06-DOMINICAN-SPANISH-ENGLISH-LINGUISTIC-PROFILE.md`), focusing on intelligibility, fluency, spoken confidence, and communicative control without optimizing for accent erasure.

## Execution Constraints

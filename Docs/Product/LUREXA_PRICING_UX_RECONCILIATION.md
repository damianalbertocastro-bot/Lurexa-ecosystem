# Lurexa Pricing UX Reconciliation

**Status:** Phase 12 architecture contract

Pricing surfaces must consume canonical commercial definitions wherever practical. Product UI must not invent a commercial rule that is absent from the canonical specification.

## Current canonical individual model

- Basic: free entry/trial experience.
- Plus: product-scoped subscription for an eligible product.
- Ultra: full Learn + Coach plus deeper cross-product adaptation and premium AI/speech.

Premium ElevenLabs is product-scoped for Plus.

## Business

Business is contract/quote based. No public fixed Business price is authorized.

The UI should communicate the customer model, organizational capabilities, negotiated usage and contact/quote flow rather than fabricate a seat price.

## Teach and institutional pricing

Teach-specific commercial scope and the final institutional capability pricing presentation are not allowed to inherit historical pricing assumptions automatically. They require explicit commercial approval before public price claims.

## Generation rule

Where a page renders pricing, it should consume a shared commercial definition rather than duplicating plan names, benefits or prices locally.

## Acceptance criteria

- Ecosystem, Learn and Coach pricing agree with the canonical commercial specification.
- Business surfaces show contract/quote semantics.
- Historical Enterprise pricing is absent from public UX.
- Teach and institutional pages do not invent prices.
- Product-scoped premium voice is represented accurately.

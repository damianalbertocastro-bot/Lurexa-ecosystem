# Human Teacher Methodology

Status: Teacher delivery specification

## Role
Human teachers extend the asynchronous curriculum through interaction, judgment, coaching, motivation and performance assessment. They should not spend live time repeating content learners have already consumed successfully.

## Live-session cycle
Retrieve -> Interact -> Apply -> Create -> Receive Human Feedback -> Set Next Target

## Teacher preparation
Before a session, teachers should receive an authorized brief containing only useful learning context:
- current level and module/unit;
- target competencies;
- recent strengths;
- recurring errors;
- vocabulary/grammar targets;
- phonetic targets;
- recent production samples when permitted;
- recommended session task.

## Teacher meeting formats
### Coaching session
Best for A1-B1 or targeted remediation:
- retrieval warm-up;
- pronunciation/phonetics;
- guided speaking;
- clarification;
- feedback and retry.

### Communication lab
Best for all levels:
- roleplay;
- problem solving;
- information gap;
- discussion/debate;
- collaborative creation.

### Performance workshop
Best for B1-C2:
- presentation rehearsal;
- writing/project feedback;
- negotiation;
- panel/seminar;
- Create & Apply assessment.

## Correction hierarchy
1. communication-blocking errors;
2. errors tied to the current objective;
3. recurring high-value learner patterns;
4. developmental errors selected strategically;
5. non-priority errors deferred.

Teachers should not correct every error in real time.

## Create & Apply assignments
Teachers may accept, modify, regenerate or create assignments. Every assignment should specify:
- objective;
- competency IDs;
- instructions;
- expected artifact/performance;
- estimated effort;
- rubric or success criteria;
- submission modality;
- due date when applicable.

## AI assistance
AI may suggest:
- session focus;
- activity sequence;
- discussion prompts;
- rubrics;
- differentiated versions;
- follow-up practice.

AI suggestions are advisory. Teacher-entered evaluations remain human evidence and should be distinguishable from AI interpretations.

## Teacher observation model
Post-session observations should be concise and evidence-oriented, e.g.:
- `maintained 4-minute conversation with minimal prompting`;
- `repeatedly omitted auxiliary do in questions`;
- `final consonant intelligibility improved after modeling`.
Avoid vague labels such as `weak English`.

## Group sessions
Group planning should use overlapping needs rather than forcing identical remediation. Lurexa Mind may surface a group focus while retaining individual evidence.

## Level adaptation
### A1-A2
High scaffolding, repetition, visual prompts, short turns, confidence-building and intelligibility.

### B1-B2
Reduced scaffolding, extended turns, discussion, repair, negotiation and fluency.

### C1-C2
Minimal scaffolding, nuanced feedback, register, argumentation, synthesis and professional/academic performance.

## Scheduling principles
Teachers can publish availability and institutions can assign sessions. Students should see preparation requirements before booking or joining.

## Session success
A strong session should produce at least one meaningful performance sample or teacher observation that can inform the learner's next experience.

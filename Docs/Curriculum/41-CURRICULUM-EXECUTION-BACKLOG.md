## Stop conditions

Pause content scaling and fix the system if:

- authors cannot map evidence to stable competencies;
- pilot learners can complete productive curricula without producing language;
- AI routinely completes learner/teacher capstone work;
- retrieval is scheduled but not tied to target competencies;
- Coach evidence conflicts with Learn evidence semantics;
- teacher evidence does not influence the learner path;
- a final capstone can be passed while a critical productive competence is unsupported;
- future-language work requires forking Core/Mind progress semantics;
- curriculum status is upgraded in the portfolio manifest without supporting runtime/pilot/capstone evidence.

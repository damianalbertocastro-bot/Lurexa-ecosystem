# Lurexa Teach Asynchronous and AI-Assisted Program Delivery

Status: Authoritative delivery standard for teacher professional development  
Depends on: `00-LUREXA-TEACH-PROFESSIONAL-DEVELOPMENT-PROGRAM.md`, `01-TEACHER-COMPETENCY-FRAMEWORK.md`, `02-TEACHER-ASSESSMENT-PLACEMENT-AND-ADVANCEMENT.md`

## 1. Core principle

Lurexa Teach should model the pedagogy it expects teachers to use.

Teacher development is asynchronous and self-paced at its core, AI-assisted throughout, and strengthened by mentor/trainer support where human observation, coaching and calibration add value.

The professional program must not become a library of recorded workshops.

## 2. Professional learning cycle

Use:

**Diagnose → Encounter → Analyze → Practice → Rehearse → Apply → Receive Feedback → Reflect → Improve → Transfer → Revalidate**

Professional learning requires performance and transfer into teaching practice.

## 3. Asynchronous core

A teacher should be able to progress independently through:

- professional scenarios;
- short research-informed explanations;
- learner-work analysis;
- lesson/material critique;
- planning/design tasks;
- AI-simulated learner interaction;
- microteaching rehearsal;
- assessment design;
- reflective analysis;
- portfolio construction;
- classroom implementation tasks where the teacher has access to learners.

## 4. AI roles in Teach

AI may act as:

- learner simulator;
- lesson-plan critic;
- language-awareness practice partner;
- feedback-quality evaluator;
- scenario generator;
- microteaching rehearsal partner;
- assessment-item critic;
- reflection coach;
- evidence organizer;
- professional reading explainer.

AI feedback must reference the relevant Lurexa Teach competency and performance criteria rather than generic advice.

## 5. AI learner simulation

Teachers should practice decisions against varied simulated learner profiles.

Simulation variables may include:

- CEFR level;
- confidence;
- recurring linguistic transfer;
- common developmental errors;
- different response quality;
- partial understanding;
- reluctance to speak;
- overconfidence;
- digital-learning difficulty.

Simulation must not claim to reproduce every real learner. Its purpose is rehearsal and reasoning practice.

## 6. Performance evidence

Teacher-development modules should produce artifacts such as:

- lesson objectives;
- activity sequences;
- feedback responses;
- assessment items;
- rubrics;
- lesson plans;
- teaching explanations;
- microteaching recordings;
- learner-work analyses;
- adapted materials;
- reflections;
- action-inquiry reports.

Knowledge quizzes contribute evidence but do not prove professional mastery alone.

## 7. Mentor/trainer layer

Human professional support is most valuable for:

- observing microteaching/classroom teaching;
- calibrating assessment judgment;
- coaching difficult interaction;
- reviewing complex curriculum designs;
- giving developmental feedback;
- supporting action research;
- validating higher-stage professional performance;
- mentoring teacher leaders/trainers.

Mentor sessions should use portfolio evidence produced asynchronously.

## 8. T1-T2 delivery priority

Initial production should focus on T1 and T2 because they serve the widest early development need.

Each module should include:

1. diagnostic scenario;
2. concise concept input;
3. analysis of strong/weak examples;
4. guided design/decision task;
5. AI simulation/rehearsal;
6. independent professional artifact;
7. feedback and revision;
8. reflection;
9. transfer-to-practice task;
10. competency evidence update.

## 9. English-development integration

Teachers who need stronger English should receive a parallel English-development recommendation rather than being held back from all pedagogical development.

Where authorized:

- Lurexa Learn develops general English;
- Lurexa Coach develops speaking/pronunciation;
- Lurexa Teach develops professional classroom language and pedagogical capability.

Evidence can inform one professional profile while competency IDs remain distinct.

## 10. Self-paced advancement

Teachers should not be forced through every module if existing evidence demonstrates competence.

Use:

- diagnostic routing;
- challenge tasks;
- recognition of prior learning;
- artifact evidence;
- mentor validation where appropriate.

Professional stages represent demonstrated capability, not seat time.

## 11. Reflective practice rule

Reflection should be evidence-based, not generic journaling.

Useful prompts include:

- What did learners actually do?
- What evidence shows learning or confusion?
- Which teacher action affected the result?
- What would you change next time and why?
- What competency does this evidence support?

## 12. Teacher-development AI safeguards

AI must not:

- certify a teacher from quiz performance;
- replace observation where demonstrated teaching matters;
- encourage copying AI-generated lesson plans without evaluation;
- present one methodology as universally correct without context;
- invent research citations or professional standards;
- hide uncertainty in professional judgments.

## 13. T1-T2 production sequence

Recommended order:

1. T1.2 Planning Lessons With a Purpose
2. T1.6 Classroom Interaction and Feedback
3. T1.3 Vocabulary and Grammar for Use
4. T1.5 Speaking and Writing
5. T1.4 Listening and Reading
6. T1.1 How Learning Happens
7. T1 capstone
8. T2.3 Assessment for Learning
9. T2.2 Pronunciation for Intelligibility
10. T2.5 Asynchronous and Blended Learning
11. T2.4 Conversation and Communicative Tasks
12. T2.1 Learner Differences and Differentiation
13. T2.6 Reflective Teaching
14. T2 capstone

This order proves Lurexa's most distinctive professional capabilities early: performance objectives, interaction, feedback, pronunciation, evidence, asynchronous design and AI-assisted practice.

## 14. Definition of success

Lurexa Teach succeeds when teachers can learn flexibly, rehearse safely with AI, demonstrate professional capability through artifacts and teaching performance, receive human coaching where it adds value, and transfer improvement into real learner outcomes.

# Lurexa Teach T2 Production Learning Blueprints — Developing Practitioner

Status: Implementation-ready professional-learning architecture

## 1. T2 outcome

T2 develops teachers who can adapt routine instruction based on learner needs, use formative evidence, teach pronunciation and communicative skills more effectively, design blended/asynchronous learning, and reflect from evidence rather than intuition alone.

## 2. Delivery model

Each T2 module remains asynchronous-first, AI-assisted, evidence-producing and compatible with mentor/expert educator support.

# Module T2.1 — Learner Differences and Differentiation

Performance outcome: adapt support, pacing, grouping or task route without lowering the intended standard silently.

Learning sequence:

case comparison → identify difference → choose adaptation → AI learner simulation → evaluate consequence → revise → transfer plan.

Evidence:

- learner-needs interpretation;
- differentiation decision;
- rationale;
- revised task preserving target competency.

# Module T2.2 — Teaching Pronunciation for Intelligibility

Performance outcome: identify communication-relevant pronunciation priorities and teach them without accent-erasure framing.

Core:

- sound/spelling awareness;
- vowels/consonants;
- final consonants/clusters;
- `-ed` and plural/third-person endings;
- word/sentence stress;
- rhythm/linking;
- correction priorities;
- Spanish/Dominican transfer used diagnostically.

AI rehearsal:

AI simulates learner pronunciation/transcript evidence and asks teacher to prioritize what matters. Teacher must justify whether to correct now, later or not at all.

Evidence:

- pronunciation diagnosis;
- focused micro-lesson;
- correction language;
- before/after learner task design.

Expert educator support:

High-value for recurring Dominican-Spanish patterns, teacher uncertainty about phonetics, and calibration of intelligibility judgments.

# Module T2.3 — Assessment for Learning

Performance outcome: design formative checks, distinguish completion from mastery, preserve first-attempt evidence and give actionable feedback.

Async sequence:

weak assessment audit → define competency → design evidence → compare recognition/production → interpret attempts → feedback decision → intervention plan.

Evidence:

- redesigned formative assessment;
- evidence rationale;
- feedback sample;
- interpretation of first vs scaffolded performance.

# Module T2.4 — Conversation and Communicative Tasks

Performance outcome: create and facilitate conversation tasks with real information/purpose rather than scripted answer exchange.

Core:

- information gaps;
- roleplay/scenarios;
- follow-up questions;
- fluency/accuracy balance;
- repair;
- teacher intervention timing.

AI simulation:

Teacher facilitates a conversation with an AI learner who may hesitate, misunderstand or use unexpected language.

Evidence:

- task design;
- facilitation choices;
- correction timing;
- post-task feedback.

# Module T2.5 — Asynchronous and Blended Learning

Performance outcome: design a learning cycle that works without continuous teacher presence and uses live time for higher-value interaction.

Core:

- async completeness;
- short interaction cycles;
- self-paced continuity;
- AI scaffolding;
- retrieval;
- teacher brief;
- live extension;
- post-session return to async path.

Performance task:

Convert a traditional 60-minute teacher-led lesson into:

1. async self-paced core;
2. AI-assisted practice/rehearsal;
3. evidence capture;
4. optional live teacher extension;
5. delayed retrieval.

AI role:

Critique completeness and learner agency. Do not simply convert lecture notes to pages.

Expert educator support:

Review difficult redesigns and identify recurring design mistakes that should become Teach exemplars.

# Module T2.6 — Reflective Teaching

Performance outcome: analyze teaching/learner evidence, identify a priority and design a specific next experiment.

Async sequence:

evidence sample → separate observation/inference → identify pattern → choose change → predict evidence → implement/simulate → compare → reflect.

Evidence:

- evidence-based reflection;
- improvement hypothesis;
- action plan;
- post-intervention interpretation.

# T2 Capstone — Improve a Learning Cycle

Teacher identifies a real or realistic instructional weakness and completes:

1. baseline evidence;
2. diagnosis;
3. redesigned sequence;
4. async/AI plan where appropriate;
5. implementation or high-fidelity simulation;
6. learner evidence;
7. analysis;
8. revision;
9. reflection;
10. next professional target.

## Advancement evidence

T2 should normally require:

- differentiation artifact;
- pronunciation teaching evidence;
- formative assessment redesign;
- communicative-task facilitation;
- asynchronous/blended redesign;
- evidence-based reflection;
- capstone improvement cycle.

## AI limits

AI cannot certify T2 from quizzes, write final professional artifacts for the teacher, or replace authentic/simulated performance evidence.

## Mentor/expert educator role

Use for:

- difficult pedagogical cases;
- pronunciation calibration;
- assessment ambiguity;
- asynchronous-design coaching;
- review of capstone reasoning;
- converting repeated teacher needs into scalable Teach curriculum assets.

## Production acceptance criteria

T2 is production-ready when its modules consistently require teachers to make defensible instructional decisions, produce evidence-bearing artifacts, rehearse practice with AI, and demonstrate improvement beyond conceptual knowledge alone.

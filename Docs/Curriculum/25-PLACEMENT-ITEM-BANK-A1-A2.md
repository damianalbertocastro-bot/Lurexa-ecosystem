# Placement Item Bank Starter — A1/A2

Status: MVP item-bank specification

## Purpose
Provide enough concrete placement content for Codex to implement a first defensible adaptive entry experience without trapping knowledgeable learners at A1.

## Design rules
- Items measure communicative knowledge in context, not isolated grammar trivia.
- Use branching by performance.
- Avoid cultural knowledge as a prerequisite for correct answers.
- Audio scripts should be recorded or synthesized with clear natural speech.
- Each item stores target competency, CEFR band, scoring key and evidence strength.

# Section B — A1 Diagnostic

## Vocabulary/Function
1. Match greeting to time: 8:00 a.m. -> Good morning.
2. Select picture/context for `family`.
3. Choose `Turn left` from a simple map instruction.
4. Select a suitable café request: `I'd like water, please.` or simpler supported chunk.

## Listening script A1-L1
A: Hi! What's your name?
B: I'm Daniela.
A: Where are you from?
B: I'm from Santo Domingo.
A: Nice to meet you.
B: Nice to meet you, too.
Questions:
- What is her name?
- Where is she from?
- Are they meeting for the first time? yes.

## Reading A1-R1
`Hi! I'm Carlos. I'm 22. I live in Santiago. I'm a university student. I like baseball and music.`
Questions:
- How old is Carlos?
- Where does he live?
- Is he a teacher? no.

## Writing A1-W1
Prompt: Write 3-5 sentences about yourself. Include your name, city/country and work/study information. You may use fictional information if you prefer.
Scoring: task completion, comprehensibility, basic `be` control.

## Speaking A1-S1
Prompt 1: `Introduce yourself. Say your name, where you are from, and whether you work or study.`
Prompt 2 follow-up: `What do you like to do in your free time?` Use only if learner can respond without excessive prompting.

## Phonetics A1-P1
Short read/imitate samples:
- Hello. My name is Marta.
- I work in Santo Domingo.
- Nice to meet you.
Observe intelligibility, final consonants, /h/, stress and phrase rhythm where relevant.

# Section C — A2 Diagnostic

## Listening A2-L1
Audio: `Last weekend, I went to Puerto Plata with my sister. We left Santo Domingo early Saturday morning and stayed near the beach. On Sunday it rained, so we visited a museum before coming home.`
Questions:
- Who went with the speaker?
- When did they leave?
- Why did they visit a museum?

## Reading A2-R1
Text: short travel message/email of 120-150 words.
Targets: main idea, detail, sequence.

## Grammar/Function A2-G1
- past simple in context;
- comparatives;
- should/can;
- present continuous vs routine;
- future plan with going to where appropriate.

## Writing A2-W1
Prompt: `Write 70-100 words about a recent weekend, trip, or activity. Say what you did, who you were with, and one thing you liked or did not like.`

## Speaking A2-S1
Prompt: `Tell me about a recent trip, weekend, or special day. What happened?`
Follow-up: `What would you do differently next time?` Use only if level permits.

## Conversation A2-C1
Scenario: You are planning a Saturday activity with a friend. Agree on place, time and one activity.
Observe ability to ask/respond, clarify and sustain 2-4 minutes with support.

## Phonetics A2-P1
Sample connected phrases and spontaneous speech. Observe clusters, sentence stress, common reductions and overall intelligibility.

# Scoring Model for MVP

## Objective items
Correct/incorrect plus response time and attempts where useful.

## Productive items
Use band descriptors:
- insufficient evidence
- below target
- emerging
- meets target
- above target

## Overall decision
Placement interpretation should consider:
- screening route;
- objective performance;
- listening/reading;
- speaking evidence;
- writing if collected;
- confidence.

Example outcomes:
- A1 start Module 1
- A1 start Module 3 after challenge validation
- A2 start Module 1
- probable B1 -> send to B1 probe once B1 item bank exists

## Early-course revalidation
Placement is provisional. First 2-3 lessons should confirm or adjust the recommendation using real coursework evidence.

# B1 Module and Unit Blueprints — Independent Communication

Status: Curriculum architecture ready for production lesson authoring  
Depends on: `11-ENGLISH-PROGRAM-MAP-A1-C2.md`, `18-B1-COMPETENCY-MATRIX.md`, `13-LEARNING-MODES-AND-AI-ASSISTANCE-ARCHITECTURE.md`

## Design rule

B1 must feel meaningfully more independent than A2. Learners should sustain connected discourse, manage interaction, explain reasons, solve familiar problems, summarize accessible information, and participate in digital/intercultural contexts with reduced scaffolding.

Every unit must specify async learning, AI role, evidence, transfer, teacher extension, retrieval, and potential expert-mentor escalation for teacher/curriculum calibration.

# Module 1 — Experiences That Shape Us

Mission: narrate experiences, explain reactions and describe why events mattered.

## Unit 1.1 — A Story With a Beginning, Middle and End
Performance: narrate a connected personal event.
Core: past simple/continuous, sequencing, time clauses.
Async: authentic-like story → structure analysis → timeline → guided retell → independent narration.
AI: ask clarifying/follow-up questions without rewriting story.
Create: 2-minute personal narrative.
Teacher: fluency + discourse coaching.

## Unit 1.2 — What Was Happening?
Performance: distinguish background/action in narration.
Core: past simple vs past continuous.
AI: diagnose meaning differences through story reconstruction.
Evidence: tense choice in meaningful narration, not isolated gap-fill only.

## Unit 1.3 — Why It Mattered
Performance: explain reaction, consequence and personal significance.
Core: because/so/when/after/before, reaction vocabulary, present perfect review.
Create: reflective audio/text story.
Teacher: support extended answers and follow-up.

# Module 2 — Relationships and Communication

Mission: discuss relationships, misunderstandings and communication choices.

## Unit 2.1 — What Do You Mean?
Performance: clarify, paraphrase and repair misunderstanding.
Core: clarification/reformulation language.
AI: intentionally introduce manageable ambiguity.
Evidence: successful repair and turn management.

## Unit 2.2 — Giving Advice Without Taking Over
Performance: describe a relationship problem and give/respond to advice.
Core: should/could/might, reasons, empathy language.
Create: advice response.
Teacher: pragmatic tone and cultural nuance.

## Unit 2.3 — When Communication Breaks Down
Performance: analyze a misunderstanding and propose a better interaction.
Core: reported speech introduction, disagreement/repair.
Mediation: relay another person's perspective simply.

# Module 3 — Work, Goals and Professional Growth

Mission: communicate experience, goals, responsibilities and workplace/study needs.

## Unit 3.1 — My Experience and Strengths
Performance: explain experience and strengths in a short professional exchange.
Core: present perfect/past contrast, achievement vocabulary.
Create: profile + interview response.

## Unit 3.2 — Plans, Goals and Possibilities
Performance: explain medium-term goals and possible outcomes.
Core: future forms, first/second conditional foundations.
AI: coaching questions that improve specificity without writing answer.

## Unit 3.3 — Professional Messages and Updates
Performance: write/respond to concise professional messages.
Online: scheduling, updates, clarification, requests.
Teacher: register and authenticity review.

# Module 4 — Media, Information and Everyday Claims

Mission: summarize accessible information, distinguish evidence/opinion and respond to claims.

## Unit 4.1 — What Is the Main Point?
Performance: identify/summarize main idea from short article/audio.
Core: reporting language, summarizing.
Mediation: relay key points to a defined audience.

## Unit 4.2 — Fact, Opinion or Interpretation?
Performance: distinguish directly supported information from opinion.
AI: challenge learner to cite evidence from supplied content.
Create: short fact-check/explainer using provided sources.

## Unit 4.3 — Responding to a Claim
Performance: state agreement/disagreement with reasons and evidence.
Core: stance, because/however/for example.
Teacher: discussion/debate extension.

# Module 5 — Travel, Culture and Intercultural Encounters

Mission: manage travel/cultural interaction and compare perspectives respectfully.

## Unit 5.1 — A Cultural Experience
Performance: narrate and reflect on intercultural experience.
Pluri: perspective-taking without stereotypes.

## Unit 5.2 — Norms, Expectations and Misunderstandings
Performance: explain a social norm and ask respectful clarification.
AI: simulate culturally different interaction without presenting one culture as default superior.

## Unit 5.3 — Guide Someone Through It
Performance: create a practical intercultural/travel guide.
Mediation: relay procedures/customs for defined audience.
Teacher: nuanced discussion and learner-specific cultural contexts.

# Module 6 — Problems, Choices and Solutions

Mission: explain problems, compare options and negotiate solutions.

## Unit 6.1 — Define the Problem
Performance: explain cause, consequence and constraints.
Core: cause/effect, modals, conditionals.

## Unit 6.2 — Compare the Options
Performance: weigh advantages/disadvantages and recommend a choice.
AI: provide changing constraints from supplied scenario.

## Unit 6.3 — Reach an Agreement
Performance: negotiate a familiar problem to a workable solution.
Conversation: agreement/disagreement, clarification, compromise.
Create: problem-solving meeting + decision note.
Teacher: live negotiation.

# Module 7 — Ideas, Opinions and Participation

Mission: express/support opinions and sustain structured discussion.

## Unit 7.1 — Build an Opinion
Performance: state position + reasons + example.
Core: stance/discourse markers.

## Unit 7.2 — Listen and Respond
Performance: respond to another view, ask follow-up, disagree politely.
AI: maintain 8–12 turn discussion with B1 language control.

## Unit 7.3 — Participate in a Discussion
Performance: sustain moderated discussion and summarize own position.
Create: opinion presentation or recorded discussion.
Teacher: live discussion with interaction rubric.

# Module 8 — Independent Communication Project

Mission: integrate narration, explanation, interaction, mediation and digital communication.

## Unit 8.1 — Choose the Message
Performance: select topic/audience/purpose and plan an applied project.
AI: planning coach, not content author.

## Unit 8.2 — Build and Improve
Performance: draft, rehearse, receive feedback, revise.
Evidence: first version and revision preserved.
Teacher: selected artifact review.

## Unit 8.3 — My Voice in English
Performance: B1 exit portfolio + conversation + mediation + writing + applied project.
AI: organize evidence and flag uncertainty.
Teacher: validate selected productive performance where available/required.

# Cross-level retrieval

B1 units must regularly retrieve:

- A2 past-event narration;
- A2 question formation;
- A2 advice/problem language;
- A2 digital interaction;
- A2 pronunciation endings/connected speech;
- A1/A2 repair strategies.

# AI progression at B1

Compared with A2, AI should:

- wait longer before scaffolding;
- request reasons/examples more often;
- tolerate longer learner turns;
- use less predictable follow-up;
- focus feedback on recurring/high-value patterns rather than every error;
- encourage self-repair;
- support planning but preserve independent production.

# Teacher role at B1

Teacher time should emphasize:

- spontaneous interaction;
- discourse extension;
- diagnostic correction;
- pragmatic nuance;
- transfer to real professional/social contexts;
- feedback on substantial productive artifacts.

# Expert educator/mentor role

Expert escalation is most useful for teacher calibration around discourse-level rubrics, fluency/accuracy balance, cultural/pragmatic cases, persistent pronunciation transfer, and recurring curriculum-design issues.

# A1 Curriculum Map — Foundations for Real Communication

Status: Production curriculum map
Primary audience: Spanish-speaking learners, Dominican-first adaptation

## Level mission
By the end of A1, learners can manage highly familiar personal and everyday communication using simple language, appropriate repair strategies and increasingly intelligible pronunciation.

## Level design
A1 contains 8 modules. Each module contains 3 units. Each unit contains 3-4 core lessons plus one integrated challenge/review sequence when needed.

The exact number of screens/activities is driven by learning needs, not symmetry.

---

## Module 1 — Hello, This Is Me
### Unit 1 — Meeting People
- Lesson 1: First Hello — greetings, names, basic social exchange
- Lesson 2: What’s Your Name? — asking/answering personal questions
- Lesson 3: Where Are You From? — countries, cities, nationalities
- Lesson 4: Meet Someone New — integrated conversation + Create & Apply

### Unit 2 — My Personal Profile
- Lesson 1: Spell It, Please — alphabet and spelling names/emails
- Lesson 2: Numbers About Me — age, phone numbers, addresses
- Lesson 3: What Do You Do? — jobs/student identity
- Lesson 4: My 45-Second Introduction — integrated oral profile

### Unit 3 — People Around Me
- Lesson 1: This Is My Family — family vocabulary and possessives
- Lesson 2: What Are They Like? — simple descriptive adjectives
- Lesson 3: Meet My People — describing one important person
- Lesson 4: Unit Mission — introduce yourself and another person

Core grammar: subject pronouns, be, possessive adjectives, basic wh-/yes-no questions.
Core phonetics: alphabet sound awareness, /h/, selected high-value contrasts, final consonants, word stress in names/countries.

## A1 capstone — My Life, My English
Portfolio/performance may include:
- 45-60 second self-introduction;
- description of an important person;
- simple daily routine;
- neighborhood map/directions;
- favorite food/activity;
- 2-3 minute supported live/AI conversation.

## Conversation rule
Every unit includes guided conversation. Every module includes at least one scenario conversation and one independent/supported performance.

## Vocabulary rule
Every lesson includes a Vocabulary Builder; every module/unit receives spaced retrieval and a Vocabulary Wrap.

## Create & Apply rule
At least one meaningful creation/performance task appears in every unit. Teacher-assigned extensions may increase depth.

## Assessment rule
Lesson quizzes are formative. Unit challenges integrate skills. Module transitions update evidence but do not independently assign permanent mastery.

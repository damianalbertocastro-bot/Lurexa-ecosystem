# English C2 Competency Matrix — Adaptive Mastery

Status: Authoritative C2 curriculum-development matrix  
Framework: CEFR C2 with Companion Volume integration

## Level mission

C2 develops highly flexible, domain-sensitive English use across unfamiliar, abstract and high-complexity contexts. Learners should interpret subtle meaning, synthesize complex sources, shift register and rhetorical strategy, mediate expert-level ideas for different audiences, and perform effectively when language demands are not fully predictable.

## Evidence expectations

C2 requires broad, repeated, high-complexity evidence. A single polished presentation or test score cannot establish C2. Evidence must include spontaneous interaction, synthesis, mediation, interpretation, register shift, unfamiliar-context transfer and sustained independent production.

## Exit evidence portfolio

C2 completion should include:

1. complex listening interpretation with subtle meaning;
2. spontaneous extended interaction in an unfamiliar or shifting context;
3. sophisticated writing adapted to purpose/audience;
4. multisource synthesis;
5. expert-to-nonspecialist mediation;
6. register transformation task;
7. broad accent/variety comprehension evidence;
8. high-complexity collaborative/online interaction;
9. independent mastery project;
10. adaptive mastery portfolio with transfer across domains.

## Mastery rule

C2 is adaptive mastery, not perfection or accent erasure. The decisive question is whether the learner can operate with exceptional flexibility, interpretive depth, synthesis, strategic mediation and register control across demanding and unfamiliar communicative conditions.

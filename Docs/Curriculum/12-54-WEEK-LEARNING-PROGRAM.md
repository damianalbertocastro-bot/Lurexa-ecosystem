# 54-Week Learning Program

Status: Delivery-model specification

## Principle
The 54-week structure is a program option and planning cycle, not a promise that every learner moves from A1 to C2 in 54 weeks.

Progression is competency-based. Calendar pace determines expected exposure and support; mastery determines advancement.

## Standard annual cycle
- 48 instructional weeks
- 4 consolidation/assessment weeks
- 2 flex/recovery weeks

## Weekly asynchronous expectation
### Flex
3-5 hours/week. Slower pathway. Core lessons, review and limited AI practice.

### Guided — recommended default
4-6 hours/week asynchronous + one 90-120 minute teacher session OR two 60-minute teacher sessions when offered.

### Intensive
5-7 hours/week asynchronous + two teacher sessions/week + increased speaking/Coach practice.

## Weekly rhythm
A recommended asynchronous sequence is:
1. Core lesson / input and vocabulary
2. Listening + phonetics
3. Grammar/reading/writing application
4. Conversation + Create & Apply
5. Retrieval/review and quiz

The exact sequence may adapt to level and learner state.

## Human meeting rule
Live sessions must not repeat the asynchronous lesson.

### One 90-minute meeting
- 10 min retrieval warm-up
- 15 min conversation activation
- 20 min targeted coaching
- 25 min communicative task/problem solving
- 15 min Create & Apply / performance
- 5 min reflection and next target

### Two 60-minute meetings
Meeting A — Language Coaching
- phonetics/pronunciation
- target error correction
- language clarification
- guided speaking

Meeting B — Communication Lab
- conversation
- roleplay
- debate/problem solving
- presentation/collaboration

## Scheduling product requirements
Teacher side:
- publish availability;
- create recurring or one-time sessions;
- set capacity and modality;
- assign session goals;
- view AI-generated learner/group brief;
- record post-session observations.

Student side:
- see eligible teacher availability;
- book/cancel within policy;
- see session objective and preparation task;
- join session;
- receive follow-up practice.

## AI session brief
Before a live meeting, Lurexa Mind may summarize authorized evidence:
- current competencies;
- strengths;
- recurring patterns;
- recent quiz/production evidence;
- pronunciation targets;
- suggested communication task.

After the meeting, teacher observations become new evidence through Core-owned contracts.

## Consolidation weeks
Every 12-13 instructional weeks, include one consolidation week emphasizing:
- spaced retrieval;
- integrated speaking/listening;
- writing/reading synthesis;
- competency revalidation;
- teacher intervention when available;
- project or performance task.

## Flex weeks
Use for illness, missed work, diagnostic remediation, teacher scheduling disruption or accelerated challenge testing. Flex weeks should not introduce essential new competencies.

## Level pacing
Lurexa does not force a universal number of weeks per CEFR level. The learner receives an estimated pathway based on placement, weekly availability, plan and demonstrated performance.

## Free-tier pacing
Free users may follow the same competency path with:
- fewer AI conversation/Coach sessions;
- fewer optional enrichment activities;
- no bundled live teacher meetings;
- slower recommended weekly sequence;
- access to designated free modules/levels.
Pedagogical quality of included content must remain unchanged.

## Completion language
Avoid: `Complete C1 in 54 weeks.`
Prefer: `Follow a 54-week structured learning cycle toward your next verified proficiency milestones.`

## Product metric
Measure learning velocity as demonstrated competency growth per active study period, not lessons clicked per week.

# English B1 Production Lesson Blueprints — Independent Communication

Status: Production authoring authority  
Depends on: `18-B1-COMPETENCY-MATRIX.md`, `23-B1-MODULE-AND-UNIT-BLUEPRINTS.md`

## 1. Purpose

B1 is the point where learners must stop relying on short routine exchanges as the dominant success condition. The production program should develop connected discourse, sustained familiar interaction, narration, supported opinion, problem solving, mediation and professional/digital independence.

This document defines lesson-level production slots without locking authors into one cultural example or script.

## 2. Standard B1 lesson behavior

A normal B1 lesson should increasingly require:

- retrieval before explanation;
- extended input rather than isolated sentences;
- note-taking/key-idea strategies;
- connected spoken/written output;
- follow-up questions;
- clarification and repair;
- reasons/examples;
- revision after feedback;
- transfer into a changed context;
- later retrieval.

AI should ask, challenge and simulate rather than compose the learner's final response.

# Module 1 — Experiences That Shape Us

## Lesson 1.1 — Tell the Story, Not the List

Mission: narrate a personal/familiar event as a connected sequence.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.SPEAK.NARRATE_EXPERIENCE`
- `EN.B1.LISTEN.NARRATIVE_SEQUENCE`
- `EN.B1.GRAMMAR.PAST_SIMPLE_CONTINUOUS`
- `EN.B1.GRAMMAR.COHESIVE_LINKING`
- `EN.B1.PHON.RHYTHM_CONNECTED_SPEECH`

Evidence: 90–120 second narrative + sequence/detail listening.

## Lesson 1.2 — What Was Happening When…?

Mission: use past simple/continuous to distinguish background and event.

Evidence:

- story reconstruction;
- guided then spontaneous narrative;
- targeted self-repair.

## Lesson 1.3 — Why Did It Matter?

Mission: explain reaction/significance, not only events.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.SPEAK.EXPLAIN_SIGNIFICANCE`
- `EN.B1.VOCAB.EXPERIENCES_EMOTIONS`
- `EN.B1.WRITE.EXPERIENCE_REFLECTION`

## Lesson 1.4 — Ask Me More

Mission: sustain a story exchange with follow-up questions.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.CONV.ASK_EXTENDED_FOLLOW_UP`
- `EN.B1.SPEAK.RESPOND_FOLLOW_UP`
- `EN.B1.PRAG.SHOW_INTEREST_FOLLOW_UP`

Coach: listener asks unpredictable but relevant follow-ups.

## Lesson 1.5 — Have You Ever / When Did…?

Mission: combine experience framing with past-event detail.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.GRAMMAR.PRESENT_PERFECT_PAST_CONTRAST`
- `EN.B1.CONV.SUSTAIN_FAMILIAR_DISCUSSION`

## Lesson 1.6 — Create & Apply: Personal Narrative Podcast

Targets:

- `EN.B1.CREATE.PERSONAL_NARRATIVE_PODCAST`
- `EN.B1.PHON.PAST_ENDINGS_SPONTANEOUS`
- `EN.B1.PHON.CHUNKING_SHORT_DISCOURSE`

Required evidence: draft/plan → recording → feedback → revised recording → later retrieval story in a changed context.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.MED.SUMMARIZE_ACCESSIBLE_INFORMATION`

- `EN.B1.MED.COMPARE_TWO_ACCESSIBLE_SOURCES`

- `EN.B1.VOCAB.MEDIA_INFORMATION`

## Lesson 4.5 — Explain It to Someone Else

Mission: mediate the main idea for a familiar audience.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.MED.RELAY_MAIN_POINTS`
- `EN.B1.MED.EXPLAIN_FAMILIAR_CONCEPT`

Target:

- `EN.B1.CREATE.EXPLAINER_FACTCHECK`

# Module 6 — Problems, Choices and Solutions

## Lesson 6.1 — Define the Problem Clearly

Mission: explain a familiar problem, causes and constraints.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.SPEAK.EXPLAIN_PROBLEM_SOLUTION`
- `EN.B1.WRITE.PROBLEM_SOLUTION`
- `EN.B1.VOCAB.PROBLEMS_SOLUTIONS`

## Lesson 6.2 — What If…?

Mission: discuss likely/hypothetical outcomes using first/second conditional foundations.

Target:

- `EN.B1.GRAMMAR.FIRST_SECOND_CONDITIONAL`

Evidence: scenario reasoning, not isolated transformations only.

## Lesson 6.3 — Compare the Options

Mission: compare feasible solutions and give reasons/examples.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.SPEAK.COMPARE_OPTIONS`
- `EN.B1.CONV.SUPPORT_WITH_REASON_EXAMPLE`

## Lesson 6.4 — Negotiate a Solution

Mission: propose, respond, compromise and confirm a simple solution.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.CONV.PROBLEM_SOLVING`
- `EN.B1.CONV.NEGOTIATE_SIMPLE_SOLUTION`
- `EN.B1.PRAG.CLARIFY_STANCE`

## Lesson 6.5 — Explain the Decision

Mission: summarize the problem, chosen solution and rationale for another person.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.MED.SUPPORT_GROUP_UNDERSTANDING`
- `EN.B1.MED.REFORMULATE_FOR_CLARITY`

## Lesson 6.6 — Create & Apply: Problem-Solving Meeting

Target:

- `EN.B1.CREATE.PROBLEM_SOLVING_MEETING`

Coach: new constraint introduced mid-negotiation; learner must adapt.

Teacher extension: live group problem-solving and negotiation observation.

# Module 8 — Independent Communication Project

## Design rule

Module 8 increases integration and independence rather than adding a large new language syllabus.

## Lesson 8.1 — Retrieve and Connect

Mission: retrieve key A2/B1 language across narrative, professional, problem and opinion tasks.

Evidence: mixed short tasks with minimal preview.

## Lesson 8.2 — Choose the Project and Audience

Mission: define a meaningful project question/product and audience.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.STRAT.PLAN_EXTENDED_RESPONSE`
- `EN.B1.WRITE.PLAN_AND_REVISE`

## Lesson 8.3 — Research and Mediate Accessible Information

Mission: use accessible sources and summarize/compare key information.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.READ.INFORMATIONAL_ARTICLE`
- `EN.B1.MED.COMPARE_TWO_ACCESSIBLE_SOURCES`
- `EN.B1.MED.SUMMARIZE_ACCESSIBLE_INFORMATION`

## Lesson 8.4 — Draft, Rehearse, Revise

Mission: create and improve connected written/spoken project content.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.WRITE.PLAN_AND_REVISE`
- `EN.B1.STRAT.SELF_REVIEW_WRITING`
- `EN.B1.PHON.SELF_MONITOR_RECURRING_PATTERN`

## Lesson 8.5 — Present and Discuss

Mission: present a familiar topic and sustain follow-up interaction.

Targets:

- `EN.B1.SPEAK.PRESENT_FAMILIAR_TOPIC`
- `EN.B1.SPEAK.RESPOND_FOLLOW_UP`
- `EN.B1.CONV.SUSTAIN_FAMILIAR_DISCUSSION`

## Lesson 8.6 — Capstone: My Voice in English

Target:

- `EN.B1.CREATE.PORTFOLIO_PROJECT`

Required portfolio:

1. connected narrative;
2. sustained interaction with repair/follow-up;
3. supported opinion;
4. professional/digital exchange;
5. connected revised writing;
6. extended listening/reading;
7. spontaneous connected-speech pronunciation sample;
8. mediation/summarization;
9. intercultural/pluricultural performance;
10. substantial Create & Apply artifact;
11. delayed A2/B1 retrieval.

## B1 Coach relationship

Coach should now require:

- longer turns;
- follow-up questions;
- reasons/examples;
- paraphrase when vocabulary is missing;
- repair without abandoning the message;
- changed scenario constraints;
- interaction beyond a scripted target sentence.

## B1 teacher relationship

Teacher-supported sessions should emphasize:

- spontaneous sustained discussion;
- narrative clarity and follow-up;
- discourse organization;
- recurring grammar/pronunciation patterns in connected speech;
- problem-solving/negotiation;
- evidence calibration for B1 exit performance.

## B1 retrieval architecture

B1 must retrieve A2 competencies under greater discourse pressure:

- past events → connected narrative;
- routine plans → goal/action discussion;
- service problems → problem solving/negotiation;
- recommendations → supported opinion;
- simple mediation → source summary/comparison;
- routine messages → professional/digital coordination.

## B1 publish gate

B1 is curriculum-complete only when:

- connected discourse is observed repeatedly;
- sustained interaction includes follow-up and repair;
- opinion evidence includes reasons/examples;
- professional/digital interaction is represented;
- mediation goes beyond copying source phrases;
- spontaneous connected-speech evidence exists;
- delayed retrieval includes A2 and earlier B1 targets;
- capstone demonstrates independent familiar-domain communication without overclaiming B2 flexibility.

# A2 Curriculum Map — Everyday Independence

Status: Curriculum production map
Level: A2 — Everyday Independence

## Level identity
A2 moves the learner from highly predictable beginner exchanges toward independent handling of familiar everyday situations. The learner should depend less on memorized scripts, connect ideas across several sentences, understand short narratives and maintain short conversations with repair strategies.

## Skill priority
Primary: speaking/conversation, listening, vocabulary retrieval.
Growing: reading, functional writing, grammar flexibility, phonetics in connected speech.

## Level outcome
By the end of A2, the learner can:
- manage familiar travel, health, work/study, shopping, leisure and social situations;
- narrate simple past events;
- express plans and preferences;
- compare options;
- give simple advice;
- sustain 2–4 minute familiar-topic conversations;
- understand short clear narratives and service interactions;
- write connected paragraphs and practical messages;
- produce increasingly intelligible connected speech.

## A2 phonetics progression
- final consonant consolidation;
- consonant clusters;
- common vowel contrasts based on learner evidence;
- -ed ending intelligibility;
- sentence stress;
- basic linking;
- weak forms/reductions in high-frequency phrases;
- more natural question/request intonation.

## A2 conversation progression
Typical target grows from 1–2 minute supported exchanges at entry to 2–4 minute familiar-topic interaction by exit.
Learner should increasingly:
- ask follow-up questions;
- clarify;
- repair misunderstandings;
- explain simple reasons;
- negotiate familiar plans.

## A2 assessment principle
A2 readiness for B1 requires multiple evidence types. Multiple-choice grammar performance alone is insufficient.

## Build order
Do not implement A2 production content before the A1 vertical learning loop and placement architecture are technically stable. Once stable:
1. A2 Module 1;
2. A2 Modules 2–4;
3. A2 Modules 5–7;
4. A2 Module 8 readiness/capstone.

This map exists now so placement, data contracts and future content generation can route above A1 without inventing A2 structure ad hoc.

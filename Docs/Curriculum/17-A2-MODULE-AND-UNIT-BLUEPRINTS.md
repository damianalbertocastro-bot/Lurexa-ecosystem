# A2 Module and Unit Blueprints — Everyday Independence

Status: Implementation-ready curriculum blueprint layer  
Depends on: `11-ENGLISH-PROGRAM-MAP-A1-C2.md`, `12-A2-COMPETENCY-MATRIX.md`, `13-LEARNING-MODES-AND-AI-ASSISTANCE-ARCHITECTURE.md`, `14-ASYNCHRONOUS-CURRICULUM-DESIGN-STANDARD.md`

## Design rule

Every A2 unit uses the same academic standard across Flex, Guided and Intensive modes.

Each unit must include:

- asynchronous self-paced path;
- purposeful AI assistance;
- speaking/listening evidence;
- retrieval from earlier learning;
- Create & Apply or transfer;
- optional teacher extension that adds value rather than repeats content.

# Module 1 — Stories From Everyday Life

Mission: describe and understand recent events and personal experiences using increasingly connected past-time language.

## Unit 1.1 — Yesterday and Last Weekend

Performance goal: describe a short sequence of recent events.

Core: past simple regular/irregular, time markers, sequencing; `EN.A2.SPEAK.DESCRIBE_PAST_EVENT`, `EN.A2.LISTEN.PAST_EVENTS`, `EN.A2.GRAMMAR.PAST_SIMPLE_REGULAR_IRREGULAR`.

Phonetics: `-ed` endings and final consonants.

Async path: story input → timeline comprehension → noticing → controlled past forms → audio micro-listening → spoken retell → delayed retrieval.

AI role: ask follow-up questions, prompt missing sequence details, provide focused `-ed`/past-form feedback.

Create & Apply: 60-90 second weekend story voice note.

Teacher extension: conversational story exchange focused on follow-up questions and intelligibility.

## Unit 1.2 — When Things Went Wrong

Performance goal: explain a simple problem that happened and how it ended.

Core: past questions/negatives, problem vocabulary, reaction phrases.

Async path: branching problem story → detail listening → reconstruct sequence → guided explanation → AI roleplay.

AI role: simulate friend/service worker asking for clarification; target repair and past questions.

Create & Apply: short problem-and-solution account.

Teacher extension: diagnose recurring question-formation and narrative breakdowns.

## Unit 1.3 — Experiences Worth Sharing

Performance goal: exchange simple experiences and explain a basic reaction/reason.

Core: experience vocabulary, past review, present perfect introduction where appropriate.

Async path: compare two personal stories → vocabulary retrieval → listening → guided conversation → independent response.

AI role: sustain 5-8 turn experience exchange at A2 level.

Create & Apply: mini audio story + response to another story.

Teacher extension: fluency-focused storytelling with selective correction.

# Module 2 — Around Town and On the Move

Mission: navigate routine travel and transportation with increasing independence.

## Unit 2.1 — Getting There

Performance goal: understand and explain basic routes, schedules and transport options.

Core: transport vocabulary, directions review, time/distance comparison.

Async path: map/schedule task → audio announcement → route choice → spoken explanation.

AI role: provide changing route conditions and ask learner to adapt.

Create & Apply: choose and explain the best route.

Teacher extension: information-gap route task.

## Unit 2.2 — Plans and Arrangements

Performance goal: coordinate time/place/transport for a simple outing.

Core: present continuous for arrangements, future time expressions, digital messages.

Async path: message thread → notice language → compose arrangement → AI negotiation.

AI role: simulate friend/colleague with constraints requiring learner to revise plan.

Create & Apply: asynchronous chat arranging an outing.

Teacher extension: spontaneous arrangement conversation.

## Unit 2.3 — Travel Problems

Performance goal: explain and solve a routine transport/travel problem.

Core: requests, clarification, delay/cancellation language.

Async path: announcement listening → identify problem → service dialogue → repair phrases → AI roleplay.

AI role: station/airport/service agent.

Create & Apply: problem-resolution simulation.

Teacher extension: unpredictable travel roleplay with pragmatic feedback.

# Module 3 — Home, Community and Services

Mission: describe communities, compare options and handle routine service needs.

## Unit 3.1 — My Home and Area

Performance goal: describe and compare homes/neighborhoods.

Core: comparatives, quantifiers, place vocabulary.

Async path: image/text comparison → listening → grammar noticing → spoken comparison.

AI role: prompt learner to justify simple preferences.

Create & Apply: neighborhood recommendation.

Teacher extension: compare two real/familiar communities.

## Unit 3.2 — Getting Things Done

Performance goal: make routine requests in familiar services.

Core: can/could, polite requests, service vocabulary.

Async path: model exchanges → politeness noticing → request builder → AI service scenarios.

AI role: vary service response and require follow-up.

Create & Apply: service interaction portfolio.

Teacher extension: correction of register/politeness and intelligibility.

## Unit 3.3 — A Problem at Home

Performance goal: explain a household/community problem and request action.

Core: problem descriptions, there was/were, need to/have to.

Async path: diagnose scenario → write message → speak to AI provider → revise after feedback.

AI role: ask clarifying questions and assess whether enough information was provided.

Create & Apply: maintenance/service request.

Teacher extension: roleplay plus written-feedback review.

# Module 4 — Health and Well-Being

Mission: communicate routine health needs, habits and advice without treating language learning as medical guidance.

## Unit 4.1 — How I Feel

Performance goal: describe common non-emergency symptoms and simple needs.

Core: body/health vocabulary, have/have got, feel, basic duration expressions.

Async path: vocabulary audio → symptom descriptions → listening → guided speaking.

AI role: language-practice receptionist/pharmacy scenario; avoid medical diagnosis.

Create & Apply: appointment/pharmacy language simulation.

Teacher extension: pronunciation and clarity coaching.

## Unit 4.2 — Healthy Habits

Performance goal: describe routines and give simple general advice.

Core: should/shouldn't, frequency, habits.

Async path: habit survey → listening → advice language → recommendation task.

AI role: prompt reasons and alternatives.

Create & Apply: personal well-being language plan.

Teacher extension: paired advice discussion.

## Unit 4.3 — Responsibilities and Appointments

Performance goal: arrange, change and explain a simple appointment need.

Core: have to/need to, scheduling, polite digital interaction.

Async path: calendar → message → listening → reschedule roleplay.

AI role: appointment coordinator.

Create & Apply: rescheduling message + conversation.

Teacher extension: pragmatic and professional-language feedback.

# Module 5 — Work, Study and Responsibilities

Mission: communicate routine responsibilities, abilities, goals and coordination needs.

## Unit 5.1 — What I Do

Performance goal: describe work/study responsibilities and routine tasks.

Core: present simple review, responsibility vocabulary, frequency.

Async path: profiles → listening → task vocabulary → spoken professional profile.

AI role: interview-style follow-ups constrained to A2.

Create & Apply: basic professional/student profile.

Teacher extension: short mock interview.

## Unit 5.2 — Skills and Goals

Performance goal: describe abilities and a short-term goal.

Core: can/could, want to/need to/going to.

Async path: goal examples → language noticing → personal plan → AI coaching questions.

AI role: help learner make the language more specific without writing the final response.

Create & Apply: 30-day study/work goal message.

Teacher extension: accountability conversation.

## Unit 5.3 — Messages That Work

Performance goal: write and respond to simple professional/study messages.

Core: basic register, requests, updates, clarification.

Async path: compare message tone → edit weak examples → write → AI feedback → revise.

AI role: evaluate clarity, politeness and completeness, not personality/style conformity.

Create & Apply: message sequence (request → clarification → update).

Teacher extension: review authentic-like communication choices.

# Module 6 — Travel and Experiences

Mission: describe experiences, plan trips and mediate straightforward travel information.

## Unit 6.1 — Have You Ever...?

Performance goal: ask and answer about life/travel experiences.

Core: present perfect introduction for experience, ever/never, past simple follow-up.

Async path: listening → form/meaning contrast → guided questions → AI exchange.

AI role: conversation partner who prompts past-simple details after experience statements.

Create & Apply: experience interview.

Teacher extension: fluency and tense-choice coaching.

## Unit 6.2 — Plan the Trip

Performance goal: compare options and make a practical itinerary.

Core: comparatives/superlatives, schedules, travel vocabulary.

Async path: research-like supplied options → compare → choose → itinerary → explanation.

AI role: challenge plan with budget/time constraints using provided information.

Create & Apply: weekend itinerary.

Teacher extension: negotiate group itinerary.

## Unit 6.3 — Pass the Information On

Performance goal: relay essential information from a notice, itinerary or message.

Core: `EN.A2.MED.RELAY_SIMPLE_INFORMATION`, key details, paraphrase simple.

Async path: notice/audio → identify essentials → relay to AI person → accuracy check → retry.

AI role: ask only for missing/unclear critical details.

Create & Apply: travel-information mediation task.

Teacher extension: human mediation with changed audience need.

# Module 7 — Entertainment, Culture and Social Life

Mission: recommend, react, compare and participate in familiar cultural/social interaction.

## Unit 7.1 — What Should We Watch or Listen To?

Performance goal: recommend familiar media and give reasons.

Core: opinion adjectives, because, should, preference language.

Async path: recommendations → vocabulary → listening → AI choice discussion.

AI role: ask learner to compare two options and defend choice simply.

Create & Apply: short recommendation recording.

Teacher extension: recommendation circle/conversation.

## Unit 7.2 — Going Out

Performance goal: invite, accept/refuse and arrange a social activity.

Core: invitation chunks, future arrangements, polite refusal.

Async path: dialogue → pragmatic noticing → chat simulation → spoken follow-up.

AI role: social partner with realistic scheduling constraints.

Create & Apply: complete social-plan exchange.

Teacher extension: spontaneous planning conversation.

## Unit 7.3 — Similar, Different, Interesting

Performance goal: describe simple cultural similarities/differences respectfully.

Core: comparison language, `EN.A2.PLURI.COMPARE_FAMILIAR_PRACTICE`.

Async path: paired cultural examples → identify observation vs judgment → compare → clarification question.

AI role: prompt perspective-taking and discourage stereotypes.

Create & Apply: brief cultural comparison.

Teacher extension: moderated intercultural discussion.

# Module 8 — Everyday Independence Challenge

Mission: integrate A2 competencies across less predictable everyday situations.

## Unit 8.1 — Solve the Day

Performance goal: move through connected practical scenarios using listening, speaking, reading and writing.

Scenarios: transport change → message → service request → scheduling problem.

AI role: orchestrate branching scenario while preserving A2 language control.

Evidence: interaction, clarification, mediation, practical writing.

Teacher extension: live multi-stage simulation.

## Unit 8.2 — Tell, Explain and Recommend

Performance goal: combine narrative, description and recommendation.

Async path: retrieval from Modules 1-7 → independent spoken/written tasks → targeted AI feedback → revision.

AI role: diagnose highest-value remaining patterns.

Create & Apply: A2 portfolio artifact.

Teacher extension: portfolio conference.

## Unit 8.3 — A2 Exit Performance

Performance goal: demonstrate everyday independence across integrated tasks.

Evidence bundle:

- listening comprehension;
- short connected speaking;
- conversation;
- practical writing;
- reading/digital interaction;
- pronunciation/intelligibility;
- mediation;
- Create & Apply;
- delayed retrieval.

AI role: administer low-stakes/simulated components and summarize evidence with uncertainty.

Teacher role: validate selected productive evidence when available/required.

# Cross-unit retrieval rules

- Every unit retrieves at least one target from the previous module.
- Every module includes at least one A1 revalidation opportunity.
- Past tense and question formation recur across Modules 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6.
- Pronunciation targets recur in spontaneous production rather than remaining isolated drills.
- Digital interaction recurs across Modules 2, 4, 5 and 7.
- Mediation appears before the capstone, not only in assessment.

# A2 production order

Recommended build sequence:

1. Module 1 fully implement and pilot.
2. Module 2.
3. Module 5.
4. Module 3.
5. Module 7.
6. Module 4.
7. Module 6.
8. Module 8.

This order proves narration, conversation, digital interaction, practical communication, mediation and teacher-supported extension early rather than delaying high-value platform behaviors until the end.

# English B1 Competency Matrix — Independent Communication

Status: Authoritative B1 curriculum-development matrix  
Framework: CEFR B1 with Companion Volume integration  
Depends on: `02-ENGLISH-COMPETENCY-MODEL.md`, `11-ENGLISH-PROGRAM-MAP-A1-C2.md`

## 1. Level mission

B1 develops connected discourse, sustained familiar interaction, narration, supported opinion, problem solving and growing independence across personal, study, work, travel and digital contexts.

## 2. Evidence expectations

B1 mastery should increasingly rely on independent production, interaction, transfer and delayed retrieval. Major productive competencies should be demonstrated in more than one context.

# Cross-module mapping

## Module 1 — Experiences That Shape Us
Narration, past systems, experience language, rhythm and story mediation.

## Module 2 — Relationships and Communication
Interaction, advice, repair, pragmatic choices, reported speech introduction.

## Module 3 — Work, Goals and Professional Growth
Professional communication, plans/goals, digital interaction, interviews.

## Module 4 — Media, Information and Everyday Claims
Reading/listening for main points, opinion/evidence, summarizing and mediation.

## Module 5 — Travel, Culture and Intercultural Encounters
Narration, cultural comparison, pluricultural competence and repair.

## Module 6 — Problems, Choices and Solutions
Conditionals, options, negotiation, problem solving.

## Module 7 — Ideas, Opinions and Participation
Argument support, discussion, discourse markers, contrastive stress.

## Module 8 — Independent Communication Project
Integrated performance, transfer, portfolio, revalidation and level-exit evidence.

# B1 exit evidence portfolio

B1 completion should include:

1. connected spoken narrative;
2. sustained conversation with follow-up and repair;
3. supported opinion presentation/discussion;
4. practical professional/study digital exchange;
5. connected writing sample with revision;
6. extended listening/reading evidence;
7. pronunciation sample in spontaneous connected speech;
8. mediation task summarizing accessible information;
9. intercultural/pluricultural task;
10. Create & Apply project;
11. delayed retrieval of selected A2/B1 targets.

# Mastery rule

B1 completion should reflect reliable independent communication across familiar domains. Occasional errors remain expected, but learners should increasingly maintain meaning, recover from breakdown, connect ideas, and transfer competencies without heavy scaffolding.

# English B2 Module and Unit Blueprints — Flexible Communication

Status: Production curriculum-development authority  
Depends on: `11-ENGLISH-PROGRAM-MAP-A1-C2.md`, `24-CURRICULUM-COVERAGE-AND-QA-GATES.md`, `25-B2-COMPETENCY-MATRIX.md`

## 1. Level design principle

B2 should feel materially different from B1.

Learners should encounter:

- longer and less predictable input;
- fewer pre-supplied phrases;
- sustained turns;
- structured argument;
- negotiation;
- register and audience choices;
- multisource synthesis;
- professional/academic interaction;
- spontaneous response;
- mediation of complex-but-accessible information.

Support remains available but should normally be reactive rather than pre-loaded.

# Module 1 — Identity, Society and Perspective

## Module mission

Discuss identity, expectations, social norms and multiple perspectives with nuance and without simplistic cultural generalization.

Core competencies:

- `EN.B2.SPEAK.SUSTAIN_EXTENDED_TURN`
- `EN.B2.SPEAK.BUILD_SUPPORTED_ARGUMENT`
- `EN.B2.READ.AUTHOR_STANCE`
- `EN.B2.VOCAB.STANCE_EVALUATION`
- `EN.B2.GRAMMAR.HEDGING_FOUNDATION`
- `EN.B2.CONV.DISAGREE_CONSTRUCTIVELY`
- `EN.B2.PLURI.INTERPRET_MULTIPLE_PERSPECTIVES`
- `EN.B2.PLURI.AVOID_SIMPLISTIC_GENERALIZATION`

## Unit 1.1 — Who Defines Identity?

Performance: explain factors shaping identity and respond to another perspective.

Learning objects:

- extended listening/readings with differing viewpoints;
- stance/noticing;
- hedging practice;
- spontaneous Coach discussion;
- short reflective argument.

Evidence:

- extended spoken turn;
- stance inference;
- structured response to another view;
- delayed reformulation.

## Unit 1.2 — Social Expectations

Performance: discuss expectations, norms and change using examples and qualification.

Focus:

- modality;
- complex comparison;
- diplomatic disagreement;
- evidence vs generalization.

Create & Apply: panel response or commentary.

## Unit 1.3 — Multiple Perspectives

Performance: compare two or more framings of one issue and mediate areas of agreement/disagreement.

Mediation:

- `EN.B2.MED.RELAY_ARGUMENTS`
- `EN.B2.MED.SUPPORT_SHARED_UNDERSTANDING`

Coach hook: perspective-switching discussion where learner must restate another position fairly before responding.

Teacher extension: moderated discussion with observation of stance, turn management and evidence use.

# Module 2 — Communication, Influence and Media

## Module mission

Analyze messaging, persuasion, audience, framing and bias across media.

Core competencies:

- `EN.B2.LISTEN.SPEAKER_STANCE`
- `EN.B2.READ.ARGUMENT_EVIDENCE`
- `EN.B2.READ.EVALUATE_SOURCE_PURPOSE`
- `EN.B2.WRITE.STRUCTURED_ARGUMENT`
- `EN.B2.CREATE.MEDIA_ANALYSIS`
- `EN.B2.PRAG.INTERPRET_IMPLICATION`
- `EN.B2.MED.SYNTHESIZE_ACCESSIBLE_SOURCES`

## Unit 2.1 — How Messages Persuade

Performance: identify audience, claim, evidence and rhetorical choice.

Evidence:

- media annotation;
- spoken explanation;
- listening stance task;
- short analysis.

## Unit 2.2 — Bias, Framing and Missing Context

Performance: compare two treatments of the same topic and explain differences in framing.

Language:

- reporting structures;
- passive range;
- evaluative vocabulary;
- hedging.

Mediation: summarize each source accurately for a defined audience.

## Unit 2.3 — Build and Critique a Message

Performance: create a short audience-specific message and critique its persuasive choices.

Create & Apply: media/campaign analysis plus revised alternative.

Coach hook: defend/critique a message under follow-up questioning.

- `EN.B2.SPEAK.ADJUST_REGISTER_BASIC_ADVANCED`

- `EN.B2.ONLINE.NEGOTIATE_TASKS`
- `EN.B2.ONLINE.ADJUST_TONE_AUDIENCE`

## Unit 3.1 — Proposals and Recommendations

Performance: explain a problem, compare options and recommend a course of action.

Evidence:

- proposal outline;
- spoken recommendation;
- follow-up questions;
- audience-aware revision.

## Unit 3.2 — Negotiation and Constructive Disagreement

Performance: negotiate priorities and reach a workable agreement.

Evidence:

- AI/peer negotiation;
- diplomatic disagreement;
- reformulation after misunderstanding;
- outcome summary.

Performance: coordinate work through professional messages/threaded discussion.

Evidence:

- task update;
- clarification request;
- response to disagreement;
- synthesized thread summary.

Coach hook: project decision meeting with shifting constraints.

Teacher extension: live negotiation observed with analytic rubric.

Explain complex-but-accessible ideas, discuss implications and evaluate options for non-specialist audiences.

Core competencies:

- `EN.B2.SPEAK.EXPLAIN_CAUSE_EFFECT`
- `EN.B2.SPEAK.EXPLAIN_SPECIALIST_TO_NONSPECIALIST`
- `EN.B2.WRITE.EXPLAIN_COMPLEX_TOPIC`

- `EN.B2.MED.EXPLAIN_SPECIALIST_INFORMATION`
- `EN.B2.STRAT.PARAPHRASE_FLEXIBLY`

## Unit 4.1 — Explain How It Works

Performance: explain a technical/scientific process to a general audience.

Evidence:

- source comprehension;
- terminology selection;
- spoken explainer;
- paraphrase check.

## Unit 4.2 — Change and Consequences

Performance: explain causes/effects and discuss probable implications.

Language:

- cause/effect;
- modality degrees;
- passive range;
- qualification.

## Unit 4.3 — Technology Choices

Performance: compare options, risks and trade-offs and make a recommendation.

Create & Apply: non-specialist explainer + recommendation.

Coach hook: explain a technology to an interlocutor who interrupts with clarification questions.

Performance: represent source ideas accurately in new wording and combine related information.

- paraphrase pairs;

# Module 8 — Flexible Communication Capstone

## Module mission

Integrate B2 receptive, productive, interactive, mediation and professional/academic capabilities under reduced support.

## Unit 8.1 — Prepare the Position

Learner selects or receives a sufficiently complex issue and:

- analyzes sources;
- identifies audience;
- develops claim/recommendation;
- plans evidence;
- anticipates counterarguments.

## Unit 8.2 — Present and Defend

Evidence:

- extended presentation;
- spontaneous Q&A;
- constructive disagreement;
- reformulation/clarification;
- discourse/prosody sample.

## Unit 8.3 — Mediate and Adapt

Learner must transform the same knowledge for a different audience or format.

Examples:

- technical → public explainer;
- long report → executive summary;
- opposing views → neutral synthesis;
- live discussion → written follow-up.

## B2 Capstone — From Opinion to Impact

Required portfolio:

1. extended listening evidence;
2. extended reading/source evaluation;
3. structured written argument/proposal;
4. spoken presentation;
5. spontaneous interaction/defense;
6. mediation/synthesis task;
7. online professional interaction;
8. pronunciation/discourse sample;
9. delayed retrieval from B1/B2 targets;
10. reflection and next-level goals.

## B2 Coach progression

Coach should move from task rehearsal toward demanding interlocutor behavior:

- interrupt appropriately;
- ask for evidence;
- challenge vague claims;
- introduce a counterargument;
- require reformulation for another audience;
- vary professional/social register.

## B2 teacher extension

Teacher-supported work should focus on:

- live negotiation;
- seminars/debates;
- oral defense;
- source synthesis discussion;
- calibration of discourse/pragmatics;
- high-value feedback on recurring advanced patterns.

## B2 retrieval architecture

Every module should retrieve:

- at least one B1 discourse/interaction competency;
- at least one earlier B2 competency in a new context;
- target language again after delay.

Module 8 serves as integrated level retrieval but does not replace earlier delayed retrieval.

## B2 publish gate

B2 is curriculum-complete only when:

- all matrix families have meaningful coverage;
- independent production is repeated across contexts;
- spontaneous interaction is observed multiple times;
- multisource synthesis/mediation is real, not selected response;
- professional/online communication is represented;
- varied-accent/natural-speed listening is included responsibly;
- capstone evidence has been calibrated;
- delayed retrieval from B1 and earlier B2 targets is present.

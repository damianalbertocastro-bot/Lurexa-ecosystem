# Unit 2 — My Personal Profile
Unit mission: Give and understand basic personal information including spelling, age/contact information, city/country and work or study identity.

## Unit competencies
- EN.A1.LISTEN.PERSONAL_INFO
- EN.A1.SPEAK.INTRODUCE_SELF
- EN.A1.SPEAK.ASK_BASIC_PERSONAL_QUESTIONS
- EN.A1.READ.FORMS_AND_PROFILES
- EN.A1.WRITE.BASIC_FORM
- EN.A1.PHON.ALPHABET_SOUND_AWARENESS
- EN.A1.PHON.NUMBER_INTELLIGIBILITY
- EN.A1.GRAM.BE_FOUNDATION

## Lesson 1 — Spell It, Please
Mission: Spell your name and understand another person's spelling.
Core vocabulary: first name, last name, full name, spell, email, letter, please, again.
Listening: names and email addresses.
Phonetics: English alphabet groups that are easily confused by Spanish speakers; distinguish letters through context and repetition rather than memorization only.
Speaking: spell first/last name and simple email username.
Conversation: AI asks for name and spelling; learner may request repetition.
Create & Apply: record a short `My name is... It is spelled...` introduction.
Quiz: alphabet recognition, spelling, repair phrase, one oral spelling item.

## Lesson 2 — Numbers About Me
Mission: Say and understand age, phone number and simple numeric information.
Vocabulary: numbers 0-100, age, phone number, address/number, zero/oh where contextually useful.
Listening: phone numbers, ages and room/address numbers.
Phonetics: teen/ty contrast, stress in thirteen/thirty etc.; clear number chunking.
Grammar/function: `I'm ___ years old`, `My phone number is...`, `What's your phone number?`
Conversation: exchange two numeric details.
Create & Apply: build a fictional contact card; protect real personal data by allowing invented numbers.
Quiz: listening discrimination and production.

## Lesson 3 — What Do You Do?
Mission: Say whether you work/study and give one simple role/job.
Vocabulary: student, teacher, engineer, doctor, nurse, driver, office, school, university, work, study.
Grammar: `I'm a/an...`, `I work...`, `I study...`; articles in high-frequency chunks.
Listening: short profiles from Dominican/Latin American and international speakers.
Phonetics: article reduction awareness and word stress in common occupations.
Conversation: `What do you do?` + one follow-up adapted to A1.
Create & Apply: 20-second profile: name + origin + role.

## Lesson 4 — My 45-Second Introduction
Integrated performance.
Learner combines:
- greeting;
- name/spelling when needed;
- city/country;
- role/work/study;
- one closing phrase.

Scaffolding sequence:
1. full template;
2. keyword cue card;
3. independent recording.

Unit vocabulary wrap: recognition -> audio -> recall -> pronunciation -> personal use.
Unit assessment: 45-second recording + short listening/form task.
Teacher extension: learners exchange profiles in pairs and teacher provides one communication strength + one priority fix.

# Unit 3 — People Around Me
Unit mission: Introduce and briefly describe family members, friends and other important people.

## Unit competencies
- EN.A1.LISTEN.PERSONAL_INFO
- EN.A1.SPEAK.INTRODUCE_OTHER
- EN.A1.SPEAK.DESCRIBE_FAMILIAR_PERSON
- EN.A1.READ.SHORT_DESCRIPTION
- EN.A1.WRITE.SHORT_PERSONAL_DESCRIPTION
- EN.A1.VOC.FAMILY_AND_PEOPLE
- EN.A1.GRAM.POSSESSIVES
- EN.A1.PHON.PLURAL_FINALS_AWARENESS

## Lesson 1 — This Is My Family
Mission: Name basic family relationships and introduce one family member.
Vocabulary: mother, father, parents, sister, brother, son, daughter, grandmother, grandfather, husband, wife, partner, family.
Cultural rule: avoid assuming one family structure. Include extended, single-parent and chosen-family-friendly examples where appropriate.
Grammar: my/your/his/her in simple chunks.
Listening: short family introductions.
Reading: simple family tree/profile.
Speaking: `This is my... His/Her name is...`
Phonetics: final sounds/plural awareness in high-frequency family words.
Create & Apply: optional family or important-people map; learners may use fictional people for privacy.

## Lesson 2 — What Are They Like?
Mission: Use simple adjectives to describe a familiar person.
Vocabulary: kind, friendly, funny, quiet, active, tall, short, young, old/older where appropriate, helpful, serious.
Grammar: adjective after `be`; avoid Spanish adjective-position transfer.
Listening: identify person from short descriptions.
Reading: profile cards.
Phonetics: adjective word stress and final consonant clarity in selected words.
Speaking: 3-4 simple sentences about one person.
Conversation: AI asks two simple follow-up questions.

## Lesson 3 — Meet My People
Mission: Combine relationship, name and description.
Input: short profiles of people from Dominican, Caribbean and international contexts.
Writing: 4-6 sentence description.
Speaking: 30-45 second description.
Phonetics: phrase chunking rather than word-by-word delivery.
Create & Apply: create an audio/photo profile card for an important person (with fictional/image-placeholder option).

## Lesson 4 — Module 1 Mission: Who I Am and Who I Know
Purpose: integrate all Module 1 competencies.

### Part A — Listen
Understand a short personal introduction that includes name, origin, work/study and one important person.

### Part B — Read
Read a simple profile and identify key information.

### Part C — Write
Complete a profile and write 4-6 sentences about self or another person.

### Part D — Speak
60-90 second supported presentation:
- greet;
- introduce self;
- say where from;
- say work/study identity;
- introduce/describe one person;
- close.

### Part E — Conversation
2-minute supported conversation with AI/teacher/peer covering at least three Module 1 functions.

### Part F — Phonetics check
Sample:
- greeting phrase;
- one spelled item;
- one numeric phrase;
- one description phrase.
Capture observations, not accent grades.

## Module 1 outcome
Completion means the learner finished the learning sequence. Competency states update only from accumulated evidence.

Recommended next action:
- ready -> Module 2;
- narrow weakness -> Module 2 + targeted review;
- widespread breakdown -> focused Module 1 re-practice;
- strong prior knowledge -> allow challenge path into Module 2.

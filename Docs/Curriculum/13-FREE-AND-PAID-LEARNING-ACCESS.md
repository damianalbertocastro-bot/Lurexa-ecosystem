# Free and Paid Learning Access

Status: Pedagogical access specification

## Governing rule
Paid access may change depth, frequency, support, AI allowance, teacher access, analytics and pace. It must not make free instructional content pedagogically inferior.

## Tier-neutral pedagogy
Every exposed lesson must satisfy the same standards for objective alignment, interaction, feedback, CEFR appropriateness and accessibility.

## Suggested product tiers
### Free
- CEFR placement/diagnostic entry experience;
- learner profile initialization;
- designated free modules or a coherent free foundation pathway;
- interactive vocabulary cards;
- core listening/reading/grammar activities;
- limited speaking recording and phonetic feedback;
- limited AI conversation allowance;
- lesson quizzes and basic mastery tracking;
- basic spaced review;
- optional separately purchased/limited teacher sessions if commercial policy allows.

### Guided
- full eligible curriculum;
- expanded AI tutoring/conversation;
- richer adaptive review;
- detailed skill profile;
- teacher meeting options or plan allocation;
- full Create & Apply tasks;
- deeper Coach integration.

### Intensive
- highest supported practice frequency;
- two-session weekly pathway where teacher supply exists;
- expanded Coach/AI allowance;
- accelerated challenge/revalidation opportunities;
- deeper analytics and intervention support.

## Free-pathway design rule
Do not create a random sampler as the only free experience. A user should be able to complete a coherent sequence and experience measurable progress.

Recommended launch strategy:
- make a substantial A1 foundation pathway free;
- allow higher-placement learners to access level-appropriate free sample units rather than forcing them into A1;
- use placement results to select the free starting experience.

## Placement access
Placement should be available to all learners because forcing knowledgeable users through entry-level content damages both pedagogy and conversion.

## Test-out access
Challenge checkpoints should not be a premium-only shortcut. Learners may demonstrate prior mastery regardless of tier. Paid tiers may provide richer diagnostics, but not artificial content locks that misstate proficiency.

## AI quota behavior
When a free AI quota is reached:
- preserve completed evidence;
- offer non-AI alternatives where possible;
- do not block core review/quiz content already included;
- clearly distinguish quota from learning failure.

## Teacher availability
Teacher meetings are an access/commerce capability, not a prerequisite for a lesson to remain pedagogically valid. Guided/Intensive plans may bundle meetings; free/Flex learners can progress asynchronously.

## Mastery integrity
Subscription status must never directly set mastery or CEFR level.

## Analytics
Free users should see meaningful progress. Premium analytics can add depth, recommendations and historical insight, but the learner should always understand their current learning direction.

## Future institutional plans
Institutions may configure access, teacher assignment and pacing, but cannot redefine competency mastery without an explicit organization-level assessment policy compatible with the curriculum framework.

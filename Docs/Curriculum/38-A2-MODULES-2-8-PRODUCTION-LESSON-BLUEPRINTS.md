# A2 Modules 2–8 Production Lesson Blueprints

Status: Production authoring authority for A2 beyond Module 1
Depends on: `12-A2-COMPETENCY-MATRIX.md`, `17-A2-MODULE-AND-UNIT-BLUEPRINTS.md`, `22-A2-MODULE-1-PRODUCTION-LESSON-BLUEPRINT.md`

## 1. Purpose

Module 1 already establishes the A2 production pattern. This document converts Modules 2–8 into lesson-level authoring slots with performance, evidence, retrieval and Coach/teacher integration.

A2 should feel less scripted than A1. Learners should combine familiar language, sustain routine exchanges, explain simple problems/reasons, and manage past/present/future reference with increasing independence.

## 2. Standard A2 lesson pattern

Each lesson should normally include:

1. retrieval from A1/earlier A2;
2. meaningful input;
3. comprehension + noticing;
4. language/pronunciation micro-focus;
5. guided production;
6. interaction;
7. Create & Apply or transfer;
8. feedback/retry;
9. delayed retrieval target.

# Module 2 — Around Town and On the Move

## Lesson 2.1.1 — Routes, Schedules and Options

Mission: understand practical route/schedule information and explain a suitable option.

Targets:

- `EN.A2.LISTEN.SHORT_ANNOUNCEMENTS`
- `EN.A2.READ.SCHEDULES_NOTICES`
- `EN.A2.SPEAK.COMPARE_SIMPLE_OPTIONS`
- `EN.A2.VOCAB.TRANSPORT_TRAVEL`
- `EN.A2.GRAMMAR.COMPARATIVES_SUPERLATIVES`
- `EN.A2.MED.RELAY_SIMPLE_INFORMATION`

Evidence:

- announcement detail extraction;
- route/schedule interpretation;
- spoken comparison;
- relay one time/place/platform detail.

Retrieval: A1 time, numbers, directions.

## Lesson 2.1.2 — Which Way Is Better?

Mission: compare familiar transport options and give a simple reason.

Targets:

- `EN.A2.SPEAK.COMPARE_SIMPLE_OPTIONS`
- `EN.A2.CONV.EXPRESS_REASON`
- `EN.A2.PHON.SENTENCE_STRESS`

Create & Apply: choose a route for a defined traveler and justify it.

Coach: traveler asks follow-up questions and changes one constraint.

## Lesson 2.2.1 — Making an Arrangement

Mission: arrange time/place/transport through conversation and digital message.

Targets:

- `EN.A2.SPEAK.MAKE_PLAN`
- `EN.A2.GRAMMAR.PRESENT_CONTINUOUS_ARRANGEMENTS`
- `EN.A2.CONV.MAKE_ARRANGEMENT`
- `EN.A2.ONLINE.ARRANGE_PLAN`
- `EN.A2.WRITE.PLAN_ARRANGEMENT`

Evidence: message exchange + live/AI arrangement.

## Lesson 2.2.2 — Change of Plan

Mission: revise an arrangement when something changes.

Targets:

- `EN.A2.ONLINE.ASK_CLARIFICATION`
- `EN.A2.STRAT.CONFIRM_UNDERSTANDING`
- `EN.A2.PRAG.APOLOGIZE_EXPLAIN`

Evidence: learner adjusts time/place and confirms final plan.

## Lesson 2.3.1 — Delay, Cancellation, Problem

Mission: understand and explain a routine travel problem.

Targets:

- `EN.A2.LISTEN.SERVICE_INTERACTIONS`
- `EN.A2.SPEAK.EXPLAIN_ROUTINE_PROBLEM`
- `EN.A2.VOCAB.PROBLEMS_SOLUTIONS`
- `EN.A2.CONV.SERVICE_PROBLEM`

## Lesson 2.3.2 — Solve the Travel Problem

Mission: interact with a service agent until the practical problem is resolved.

Evidence:

- multi-turn roleplay;
- clarification;
- request;
- solution confirmation.

Create & Apply: **Get Me There** travel problem simulation.

Teacher extension: rotating transport-service roleplays with unpredictable but routine changes.

# Module 8 — Everyday Independence Challenge

## Design rule

Introduce minimal new language. Increase integration, independence, response length, variability and retrieval distance.

## Lesson 8.1.1 — Tell What Happened

Mission: narrate a recent event and answer follow-up questions.

Evidence:

- connected past account;
- `-ed`/past intelligibility sample;
- follow-up interaction.

## Lesson 8.1.2 — Explain a Problem

Mission: explain a practical problem, request action and clarify details.

Integrates Modules 2–4.

## Lesson 8.2.1 — Make and Change a Plan

Mission: coordinate a plan, adapt to a change and confirm final details.

Integrates Modules 2, 5, 7.

## Lesson 8.2.2 — Recommend and Compare

Mission: compare familiar choices and recommend one for a defined person/purpose.

## Lesson 8.2.3 — Relay the Information

Mission: mediate practical information from a short notice/message/schedule.

## Lesson 8.3.1 — Everyday Independence Conversation

Multi-scenario conversation requiring:

- past experience;
- plan;
- problem;
- reason;
- clarification;
- follow-up;
- service/social interaction.

## Lesson 8.3.2 — Capstone: Living More Independently in English

Portfolio:

1. spoken past experience;
2. routine problem/service interaction;
3. plan/arrangement interaction;
4. connected writing;
5. practical listening;
6. functional/digital reading;
7. pronunciation sample;
8. mediation task;
9. recommendation/Create & Apply artifact;
10. delayed A1/A2 retrieval.

## A2 Coach relationship

Coach should move beyond A1 scripts by:

- asking follow-up questions;
- changing routine constraints;
- requesting simple reasons;
- prompting repair rather than immediately supplying language;
- revisiting past/plans/problems across new contexts.

## A2 teacher relationship

Teacher sessions should emphasize:

- connected storytelling;
- routine problem solving;
- question formation;
- past endings;
- follow-up interaction;
- practical digital/professional communication;
- evidence gaps identified by Mind.

## A2 retrieval architecture

Cross-level retrieval must include A1:

- introductions/details inside new situations;
- times/numbers in arrangements/services;
- present-simple routines contrasted with past/future;
- basic transactions extended into problems;
- directions extended into travel.

A2 targets should recur across modules and in Module 8 rather than being isolated topic skills.

## A2 publish gate

A2 is ready only when:

- all major productive competencies have evidence in at least two contexts where specified by the matrix;
- past/present/future reference is evidenced in communication;
- service/problem interactions are real multi-turn tasks;
- digital communication and mediation are implemented;
- connected-speech/pronunciation evidence exists;
- delayed retrieval includes A1 and early A2 targets;
- the capstone shows routine independence without pretending B1 discourse complexity.

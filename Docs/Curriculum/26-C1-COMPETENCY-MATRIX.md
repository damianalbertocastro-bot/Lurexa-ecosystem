# English C1 Competency Matrix — Precision, Identity and Global Perspective

Status: Authoritative C1 curriculum-development matrix  
Framework: CEFR C1 with Companion Volume integration

## Level mission

C1 develops nuanced, precise and strategically flexible communication across complex professional, academic, cultural and public contexts. Learners should synthesize demanding input, manage register deliberately, mediate complex ideas and perspectives, facilitate interaction and communicate uncertainty responsibly.

## Exit evidence

C1 completion should include complex listening/reading synthesis, extended oral argument, facilitated discussion, research-informed writing, strategic mediation, professional/online collaboration, pronunciation/rhetorical delivery, and a substantial Create & Apply capstone.

## Mastery rule

C1 requires reliable precision and flexibility across demanding contexts, but not uniform perfection. Evidence must show strategic adaptation, synthesis, pragmatic control and transfer beyond rehearsed topics.

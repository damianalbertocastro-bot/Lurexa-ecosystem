# Curriculum Persona

## Mission

Design learning experiences that are clear, progressive, inclusive, and measurable.

## Focus

- Learning objectives and learner journeys
- Content sequencing, practice, and assessment
- Accessibility and evidence of learning

## Working principles

- Begin with the learner, their context, and the target capability.
- Make objectives observable and assessments aligned to them.
- Build from simple concepts to applied practice with timely feedback.

# CPO Persona

## Mission

Own product strategy and turn customer needs into an effective product roadmap.

## Focus

- Problem discovery and product-market fit
- Product requirements, prioritization, and roadmap planning
- Outcomes, adoption, retention, and product quality

## Working principles

- Validate the problem before prescribing a solution.
- Define success metrics and non-goals for each initiative.
- Balance customer value, business value, and delivery effort.

# CEO Persona

## Mission

Guide company-level strategy, priorities, and trade-offs for Lurexa.

## Focus

- Vision, positioning, and business model
- Customer value and sustainable growth
- Strategic priorities, risks, and resource allocation

## Working principles

- Start with the customer and the desired business outcome.
- Prefer clear, measurable goals over activity for its own sake.
- State assumptions, risks, and decisions explicitly.

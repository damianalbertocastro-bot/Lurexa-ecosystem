# Marketing Persona

## Mission

Build awareness, demand, and durable customer relationships through clear, relevant communication.

## Focus

- Audience insight, positioning, and messaging
- Campaign strategy, content, and channel selection
- Conversion, retention, and brand consistency

## Working principles

- Lead with the audience's need and the value Lurexa provides.
- Use specific claims that can be supported.
- Define a primary message, a clear call to action, and a success metric.

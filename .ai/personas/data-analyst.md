# Data Analyst Persona

## Mission

Turn reliable data into useful decisions while protecting customer privacy and communicating uncertainty.

## Focus

- Metric definitions, data quality, and exploratory analysis
- Experiment design, reporting, and decision support
- Clear visualisation and interpretation of results

## Working principles

- Define the decision, metric, population, and time period before analysing.
- Check data completeness, bias, and confounding before drawing conclusions.
- Distinguish observed facts, inferences, and recommendations.

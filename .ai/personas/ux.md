# UX Persona

## Mission

Create simple, accessible, trustworthy experiences that help people complete meaningful tasks.

## Focus

- User research, flows, interaction design, and information architecture
- Accessibility, usability, and content clarity
- Prototyping and validation with representative users

## Working principles

- Solve the user’s underlying job, not just the requested feature.
- Make system state, consequences, and recovery paths clear.
- Use progressive disclosure and reduce cognitive load.

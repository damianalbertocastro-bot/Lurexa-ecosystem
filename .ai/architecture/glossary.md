# Lurexa Architecture Glossary

Last updated: 2026-08-17

## Lurexa Learning Technologies
Parent company and master business identity for the Lurexa ecosystem.

## Lurexa Core
Shared trusted technical/platform foundation. Owns or governs identity, authentication, authorization, permissions, trusted learner records, persistence, cross-product data contracts and shared platform services.

## Lurexa Mind
Shared AI and learning-intelligence layer. Interprets authorized learning evidence and supports personalization, adaptation, recommendations, interventions, tutoring/coaching and cross-product learning intelligence.

## Learner Model
The persistent evolving representation of a learner across the Lurexa ecosystem. It spans Core-governed trusted evidence/state and Mind-produced interpretations. It is not a separate product profile or an ungoverned Mind-owned database.

## Learning evidence
Observed or structured information generated by a learning experience, such as activity outcomes, assessment responses, progress, speaking observations or successful corrections.

## Derived interpretation
A conclusion or estimate produced from evidence, such as likely proficiency, recurring-error pattern, pronunciation target or recommended intervention.

## Learner Context Contract
A future governed contract through which an authorized product receives only the learner context necessary for a particular experience.

## Learning Evidence Contract
A future governed contract through which products submit structured learning evidence for trusted persistence and later interpretation.

## Derived Observation Persistence Contract
A governed boundary through which approved Mind outputs can become persistent trusted learner state under Core ownership.

## Cross-product learning loop
The two-way flow in which products generate evidence, Core governs trust/persistence, Mind interprets, and authorized products adapt future experiences.

## Lurexa Learn
Learner-facing LMS and structured learning experience.

## Lurexa Coach
AI-powered English speaking and pronunciation product. First deep linguistic specialization: Dominican Spanish speakers learning English. Its goal is intelligibility, naturalness, fluency, pronunciation refinement and confidence — not accent erasure.

## Lurexa Teach
Teacher-facing product.

## Lurexa Admin
Institutional and administrative product.

## Lurexa Insight
Analytics, intelligence and reporting product. `Lurexa Analytics` and `Lurexa Insights` are obsolete product names unless explicitly reintroduced.

## Lurexa Studio
Content and learning-experience creation product.

## L1 linguistic profile
A reusable representation of predictable first-language-to-English transfer patterns used pedagogically by Mind/Coach. Dominican Spanish is the first deep profile, not the only future profile.

## Thesis prototype
Earlier validation/reference artifact. It is not the active production architecture.

## Commercial ecosystem
The active scalable, production-grade, multi-product Lurexa platform and business direction.
